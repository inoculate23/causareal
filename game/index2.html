Causa Real

LOS GORDOS DE LA LEY

Causa Real is a raw, unapologetic rap collective from Lima, Peru, carving out space for social critique and street-level truth. Their name — "Real Cause" — signals their mission: music as testimony, not entertainment.

Blending hard-hitting lyricism in Spanish and English, they channel the frustrations of a generation caught between corruption, inequality, and cultural erasure. Los Gordos de la Ley is a blistering indictment of those who bend justice for their own ends.
